Delete your account

The Balance Board Game · Developer: omriarbel

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open The Balance Board Game.
  2. On the main menu, tap your avatar (next to Calibrate).
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Send an email to balanceboardgame@gmail.com from the email address of your account (for "Continue with Google" accounts, your Google email address), with the subject "Delete my Balance Board Game account". We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

From our database, permanently: your login (email address, and your password or the link to your Google account, with the Google account details), username, country, personal best and its leaderboard entry, and all usage data linked to your account. That is every app session in which you were signed in, including the parts of that session before you signed in or after you signed out, with its game rounds and events.

Our provider's service logs (which include IP addresses) and any database backups are deleted within 7 days.

When you delete your account in the app, the app also removes your account's usage records still waiting on your phone and starts using a new random installation ID.

When we delete your account after an email request, the app on your phone is not told. Usage records still waiting on it may be uploaded later without any link to your account, as guest data. To remove them, uninstall the app, or switch off Share usage statistics (see below) on that phone.

What is kept, and for how long

Usage records from app sessions in which you were never signed in (for example, play before you created your account) are not linked to any account and are not deleted with it. Because nothing links them to you, we cannot find them to delete them early. We delete old usage data by hand about once a month, so they are deleted at most 13 months after their last activity. To delete them too, switch off Share usage statistics while still signed in, before deleting your account.

Data stored on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Delete usage data without deleting your account

On the main menu, tap Privacy and switch off Share usage statistics. The app stops recording, and our server deletes the usage data sent from your phone without an account or while signed in to the account you are signed in to at that moment. Usage data recorded on your phone while signed in to a different account is deleted only with that account.
